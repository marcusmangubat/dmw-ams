STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

Your G2G Journey with DMW

Safe, ethical, and no-fee overseas employment through government-to-government partnerships.

1

Register on the DMW Portal

Create or log in to your account. Complete your profile with personal info, education, work experience, skills, and job preferences.

2

Browse & Select G2G Vacancies

Visit the G2G Job Opportunities page to view current Recruitment Specification Forms (RSF) from partner countries. Filter by country, position, or control number and choose roles that match your skills.

3

Apply & Upload Documents

Submit your application through the G2G portal or specified process. Upload required documents such as valid passport, resume, certificates (TESDA/training), employment history, and any language proficiency proofs.

4

Screening, Shortlisting & Interview

DMW and/or the partner country's agency reviews applications. Qualified candidates may undergo skills assessment, interviews (virtual or in-person), and further evaluation. Stay updated via your DMW account.

5

Receive Offer & Complete Processing

If selected, receive a job offer. Undergo medical exam, contract validation, pre-departure orientation (if required), and final document processing. DMW issues your Overseas Employment Certificate (OEC) for deployment.

6

Deployment & Continued Protection

Depart safely with full government protection. DMW provides on-site welfare support through embassies/consulates and 24/7 hotlines. Enjoy ethical employment with no placement fees.
